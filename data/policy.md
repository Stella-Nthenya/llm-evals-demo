Northbean Coffee (fictional shop) support facts
- Returns: unopened items within 30 days of delivery. Opened coffee cannot be returned.
- Refunds: 5 to 7 business days to the original payment method.
- Shipping: standard 3 to 5 business days, $6. Free over $50. Ships only to the US and Canada.
- Subscriptions: can be paused or cancelled any time before the next billing date from the account page.
- Faulty grinders: 12-month warranty; contact support@northbean.example with a photo and order number.
- Order status and account changes: the assistant cannot see orders or accounts.
